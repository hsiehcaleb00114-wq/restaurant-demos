La MaisonA little Paris, 32 seats

Chef Adrien spent ten years in the bistros of Lyon. He says the idea is simple: "Generous plates, rich sauces, and wine cheap enough that you order another glass."

In 2018 he and his Taiwanese wife Yuki opened a 32-seat bistro on the ground floor of an old Xinyi apartment building. The chalkboard menu changes every week, and the wine list keeps growing. The cellar now holds 180 wines from small French growers.

No white tablecloths here. There's a zinc bar and red velvet chairs, and guests often stay until closing.

— Adrien & Yuki
